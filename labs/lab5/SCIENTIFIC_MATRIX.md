# Transversal Lab — Lab 5 / Scientific Matrix

## Focus
Reflexive-regulatory component.

## Source basis
The current II–III chapter materials describe reflection and self-assessment as linked to professional growth; they emphasize analyzing one's activity, identifying causes of errors/difficulties and improving subsequent activity. The experimental section states that four components are used for diagnosis and that practical tasks, observation sheets, expert assessment, electronic portfolios and reflective reports are among the assessment tools.

## Methodological note
This module produces observable evidence for a future integrated diagnostic system. It does **not** assign a final scientific score and does not establish validated thresholds or weights.
