# Scientific Matrix — Lab 3 v0.1

| Stage | Student action | Component | Indicator evidence | Observable evidence | Boundary |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | Chooses an opening that gives a participant room to speak | Social-communicative | understanding position; active listening | open invitation rather than judgment | One choice alone does not prove stable competence |
| Opening rationale | Explains why the opening is appropriate | Social-communicative + cognitive | adaptation of communication; interpretation | written rationale | Requires expert review for quality |
| Clarification | Chooses a question focused on a concrete event | Social-communicative + cognitive | clarifying questions; feedback; professional interpretation | selected question and rationale | Does not measure real-time spoken interaction |
| Alignment | Chooses a formulation that acknowledges both positions and defines a shared next action | Social-communicative + reflective | alignment of positions; constructive problem resolution; use of feedback | selected formulation and rationale | One case cannot establish transfer/generalization |
| Completion | Reflects through the rationale for the joint action | Reflective-regulatory | rechecking decision; improving next action | written explanation | Full reflective competency needs additional tasks |

## Four-component continuity
Lab 3 does not replace the four-component model. It intentionally gives stronger observable evidence for the social-communicative component while allowing limited cross-component evidence.

## Assessment rule
Do not turn the evidence tags into a numerical scientific score unless the full dissertation diagnostic system defines and validates the thresholds/weights.
