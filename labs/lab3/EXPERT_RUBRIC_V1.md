# Expert Rubric — Lab 3 v0.1

Use this as a working observation aid, not as a validated scale.

| Dimension | Observable focus |
|---|---|
| Understanding the participant | Does the student avoid premature judgment and make space for the participant's position? |
| Active listening | Does the chosen opening show an intention to hear the participant before deciding? |
| Clarification | Does the student ask for a concrete event rather than forcing a ready-made interpretation? |
| Adaptation | Does the explanation show awareness of the interlocutor and context? |
| Alignment | Does the proposed formulation acknowledge multiple positions and move toward a shared action? |
| Reflection | Does the student use the new understanding to shape the next action? |

Suggested expert use: qualitative judgement and comments first; numerical conversion only after the complete diagnostic framework is agreed.
