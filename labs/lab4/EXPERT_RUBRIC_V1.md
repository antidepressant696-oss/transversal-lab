# Expert Rubric v1 — Lab 4

This is a working expert rubric, not a validated psychometric instrument.

## Primary focus: Digital literacy

Observe whether the student:
1. searches digital information for a pedagogical need;
2. checks source/content reliability;
3. selects a digital tool for the didactic task;
4. critically evaluates an AI-generated result;
5. considers digital ethics and responsibility where relevant;
6. justifies the digital choice in relation to the teaching task.

## Supporting evidence
- Cognitive: identifies relevant information and checks the basis for a pedagogical decision.
- Social-communicative: adapts a digital resource to learners’ age and needs.
- Reflexive-regulatory: re-checks the decision and changes the next action where needed.

## Do not infer
Do not convert one case result into a final competence level. Numerical thresholds and weights must be established for the complete diagnostic system.
