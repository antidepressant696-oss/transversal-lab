# Lab 2 — Diagnostic Record v1.0

The browser stores a completed Case 01 as JSON under `tl-lab2-case01`.

The record contains:

- `responses` — what the student actually selected/wrote;
- `evidence` — system-captured evidence or candidate indicators for expert review;
- `expertReview` — reserved structure for future teacher/expert confirmation;
- `completedAt` — completion timestamp.

The site does not convert these observations into a scientific score.
